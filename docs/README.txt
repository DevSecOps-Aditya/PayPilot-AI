# 💰 PayPilot AI

PayPilot AI is an intelligent financial copilot that helps users manage their personal finances with confidence. It provides smart insights into spending habits, budgets, subscriptions, savings goals, and overall financial health through a simple conversational experience.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 Expense Tracking
- 💸 Budget Management
- 🔄 Subscription Monitoring
- 🎯 Savings Goal Tracking
- 📈 Spending Analysis
- 🤖 AI-Powered Financial Insights
- 💡 Personalized Recommendations

## 🚀 What PayPilot AI Can Do

PayPilot AI helps users:

- Understand where their money is being spent
- Identify unnecessary recurring expenses
- Monitor budgets in real time
- Build healthier financial habits
- Plan and achieve savings goals faster
- Make informed financial decisions with actionable insights

## 🧠 How It Works

PayPilot AI analyzes financial data and transforms complex numbers into clear, easy-to-understand guidance. Users can ask natural questions such as:

- "Where did I spend the most money this month?"
- "Which subscriptions can I cancel?"
- "How much can I save if I reduce my discretionary spending?"
- "Am I on track to reach my savings goal?"

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to make personal finance simple, accessible, and intelligent for everyone. By combining analytics, goal tracking, and AI-driven recommendations, PayPilot AI empowers users to take control of their finances and achieve their financial goals with confidence.
